# Voice direction

- English, neutral international/American accent.
- Deep, mature male documentary delivery; calm, curious and precise.
- Target pace: 150 words per minute; measured estimate is 54.4 seconds.
- Pause briefly after the opening question and after “the bridge keeps changing”.
- Emphasize “lengthen, widen and shift”, “local”, and “temporarily unavailable for foraging”.
- Avoid implying ants consciously calculate the colony-wide trade-off.
