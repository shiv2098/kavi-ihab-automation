## Alternative research
- Flamingo posture: Chang & Ting (2017), Biology Letters. Cadaveric flamingos could passively support body weight in a stable one-leg posture; live birds showed reduced sway when quiescent. This supports a passive mechanical contribution, not a complete explanation of why flamingos choose the posture. https://doi.org/10.1098/rsbl.2016.0948
- Gecko adhesion: Autumn et al. (2002), PNAS. Direct experiments supported van der Waals adhesion by gecko setae rather than capillary adhesion as the primary explanation in tested conditions. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.192252799
- Woodpecker impact: a 2022 Current Biology study found the cranial skeleton functions as a stiff hammer rather than a shock absorber in the studied species; modelled brain inertial loading remained below primate concussion thresholds. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cub.2022.05.052
