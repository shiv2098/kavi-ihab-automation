# Detailed 9:16 visual prompts

All supplied scene graphics are original code-generated diagrams. These prompts are production direction for optional later footage; do not run them through a paid-credit generator.

1. Hook: cinematic macro of a forest-floor gap between two rough twigs, a living bridge of small dark Eciton army ants linked body-to-body across it, other ants crossing above, damp leaf litter, shallow depth of field, natural scale, vertical 9:16.
2. Structure: close macro on individual ants attached into a continuous living chain, six-legged insect anatomy, segmented bodies and antennae, a line of nestmates moving over the structure, no human-like limbs or literal rope.
3. Detour: overhead forest-floor trail around a hole, curved long route in muted amber and direct bridge route in cyan, ants moving in a single direction.
4. Migration: two time-separated views of the same bridge, first near the bend and later shifted toward the main trail axis; show it lengthening and widening through workers joining/leaving, not sliding as a rigid object.
5. Local joining: high-contact side of bridge receives more passing-ant contacts; a few workers attach. Label “likely local mechanism”; no central queen or commander directing construction.
6. Local leaving: low-contact outer side loses a few workers while inner busy side gains them; show local contact differences rather than conscious decisions.
7. Payoff: wide cinematic view of a living ant bridge with traffic flowing over it; overlay shorter travel versus workers tied up as supports.
