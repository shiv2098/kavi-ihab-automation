## Description
Ever stepped out of a dim room into bright sunlight and felt a sneeze coming? That is the photic sneeze reflex, sometimes called ACHOO syndrome. Research suggests it affects about one in four people, but scientists have not settled on one exact neural pathway.

An EEG study found stronger visual-cortex responses in people with the reflex. A separate genome-wide study found genetic variants associated with it in a Chinese population of 3,417 participants. Neither study fully explains how the reflex works.

Research:
- Trinkl et al. (2025): https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00221-024-06988-4
- Langer et al. (2010): https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0009208
- Wang et al. (2019): https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-019-41551-0
