## Description
A popcorn kernel does more than burst. High-speed video revealed a tiny projection of starch that presses against the pan, rebounds and launches the flake. Researchers also synchronized sound and video, proposing that the familiar “pop” comes from released water vapour before the jump.

Research: Virot & Ponomarenko (2015), https://doi.org/10.1098/rsif.2014.1247 ; Purdue Food Science (2020), https://ag.purdue.edu/news/2020/02/whats-under-the-shell-of-this-popular-snack.html
