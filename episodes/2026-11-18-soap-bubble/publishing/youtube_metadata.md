## Description
Why does a soap bubble shimmer with rainbow colours? Its thin liquid film reflects light from two surfaces. Those reflected waves interfere, reinforcing some wavelengths and cancelling others. As the film thickness changes, the colours shift — and the thinnest patches can look dark.

This is thin-film interference, the same wave effect used in optical coatings.
