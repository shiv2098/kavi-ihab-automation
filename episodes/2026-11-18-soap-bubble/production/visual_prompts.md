# 9:16 visual prompts and art direction

All supplied PNGs are original code-generated illustrations. No AI-video generator, stock footage or third-party images were used.

1. Hook: Extreme macro of a single soap bubble against a dark navy studio background, translucent spherical film with shifting iridescent bands, cinematic specular highlights, no pigment, no text, no watermark.
2. Thin film: Clean scientific cutaway of air / soap-water film / air. Film is extremely thin relative to bubble diameter; label “schematic, not to scale”.
3. Two reflections: White incident light partly reflects at the outer air-film interface; the rest enters the film, reflects at the inner interface and exits beside ray 1. Distinguish both paths.
4. Interference: Two sinusoidal light-wave traces. One panel shows peaks aligned (constructive); another shows opposite phase (destructive).
5. Thickness: Soap-film bubble with spectral bands and a downward gravity arrow indicating liquid drainage. Do not claim all bubbles have identical colour order.
6. Black film: Very thin region appears dark because reflected visible wavelengths cancel strongly. It is not black pigment or dirt.
7. Payoff: Iridescent bubble with “THICKNESS + ANGLE = COLOUR” and “LIGHT WAVES ADDING — AND CANCELLING”.

Motion: gentle 3–5% push-in/pan per scene. No rapid flashing. Keep captions clear of the bottom 15%.
