## Description
Thousands of bats can pour out of a cave while their echolocation calls overlap. Researchers studying greater mouse-tailed bats found severe acoustic masking that dropped as the animals spread out. A 2026 computer model suggests that combining information across several calls and using simple local movement rules could help bats navigate crowded exits.

The model is a proposed explanation, not proof that every real bat follows the same sequence.

Research:
- Goldshtein et al. (2025), PNAS: https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2407810122
- Mazar & Yovel (2026), eLife: https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.105571
- Max Planck Institute of Animal Behavior (26 March 2025): https://www.mpg.de/24391812/0326-ornr-scientists-solve-cocktail-party-mystery-of-bat-echolocation-987453-x
